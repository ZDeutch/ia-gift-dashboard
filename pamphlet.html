    How Trump Accounts work

    • $1,000 to start. The U.S. Treasury seeds an account for every American child born January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2028.
    • In the child’s name. A parent or guardian is the sole custodian until the child turns 18.
    • Room to grow. Family, friends, employers and philanthropists can add up to $5,000 a year.
    • Invested for the long term. Balances grow over time into assets for education, housing, or a business.

    Method and sources

    Children are U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 5-Year Estimates (2020–2024) of children under 18 for whom poverty status is determined (table B17024), read at the ZIP Code Tabulation Area level, the same universe reported by the Invest America gift planning dashboard. Income eligibility uses each ZIP’s median family income (table B19113): a ZIP qualifies when its median is at or below the selected limit; medians the Census suppresses stay in, and medians top-coded at “$250,000 or more” qualify only when the limit is above $250,000. Children reached = children in the selected ages and qualifying ZIPs × the claim rate, rounded per ZIP; the commitment = children reached × gift per child. Single-year age ranges take an even share of each Census age band (under 6, 6–11, 12–17). Counties and cities follow the 2020 Census ZIP-to-county and ZIP-to-place relationship files. A ZIP passing the income filter is not a determination of legal eligibility. ACS figures are survey estimates with sampling error, largest for small areas; validate before formal decisions.
